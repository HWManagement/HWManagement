    Privacy Policy

    HW Entertainment LLC · Last updated August 13, 2026

    This policy describes how HW Entertainment LLC (“HW Entertainment,” “we,” “us”) handles information on this website. It is written for customers and advertising reviewers. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

    Who we are

    HW Entertainment LLC licenses verses and related entertainment services. Contact us at hw_management@yahoo.com. We reply within 24 hours on business inquiries.

    Information we collect

    When you use a booking or contact form, we collect the details you submit. That typically includes first name, last name, email, phone, company, budget range, desired artist, and service type. Hidden fields may also pass campaign tags (for example utm_source) so we know which page sent the request.

    Forms on this site post to Formspree (formspree.io). Formspree receives the submission, stores it according to its own terms, and emails it to HW Entertainment LLC. We do not host a private customer database on this static site.

    The site may load fonts, a Meta (Facebook) pixel if configured, and standard server or GitHub Pages logs (IP address, user agent, referring URL). We do not run account logins on this site.

    How we use information

    • To reply to your request — we aim to reply within 24 hours with availability and pricing.
    • To prepare licensing, clearance, and payment paperwork if you book.
    • To understand which ads or pages sent a request.
    • To operate, secure, and improve the site.

    We do not sell personal information

    HW Entertainment LLC does not sell personal information. We do not share form submissions with third parties for their marketing. We share data only with processors needed to run the site and fulfill a booking (for example Formspree for form delivery, payment or contract tools if a deal closes, and email).

    Cookies and ads

    If a Meta Pixel ID is installed, Meta may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure ad performance. Browser settings can limit cookies. Google Ads, if used later, may also receive standard click and conversion signals. This landing site itself does not require an account or cookie consent wall to read our catalog.

    Retention

    We keep inquiry records as long as needed to respond, complete a license, and meet ordinary business and tax records. You can email hw_management@yahoo.com to ask what we hold or to request deletion of a form submission we still control.

    Children

    This site is for adults booking commercial music licenses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy: hw_management@yahoo.com. We reply within 24 hours. Related pages: Terms and Request Form.
