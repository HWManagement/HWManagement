Sample license structure
  1. 01

    Hear it & read it

    Every catalog listing has a 15-second watermarked preview, so you hear the actual recording first. Then read how the license is structured: rights, territory, term, exclusivity, name use, splits, and what happens if a distributor removes the release. Sample structure.

  2. 02

    Confirm, then pay

    We confirm the recording, license type and credit terms with you directly. There is no online checkout on this site; payment is arranged directly with HW Entertainment LLC.

  3. 03

    Receive paperwork & files

    Your signed license agreement, the split sheet, and the audio files (stems where your license includes them).
