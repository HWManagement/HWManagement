Custom

You want something new from a specific artist: a custom verse or an introduction. We contact the artist’s team on your behalf. No guarantee. Quote on request.

  1. 01

    Tell us the artist and the song

    Who you want, what the record is, your budget and your timeline.

  2. 02

    We contact the artist’s team

    We reach out to the artist’s management or team with your request.

  3. 03

    They decide

    The artist’s team decides whether to do it, and on what terms and price. Some will say no. We let you know what they say.

  4. 04

    Terms in writing

    If they agree, you get their terms and price, and the deal is documented in a written license.

Price

Quote on request. It depends on the artist and what they agree to.

No guarantee

We can make the request. We can’t promise any artist will accept, and we never present a request as a done deal.
