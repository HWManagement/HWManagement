HW Entertainment LLC / HW Management. We’re in Los Angeles.

Founder & Owner, HW Entertainment LLC / HW Management

Herman Watson

Herman Watson is a music industry operator and the founder of HW Entertainment LLC. He has hands-on experience managing and developing artists, including his earlier work as manager for Rappin’ 4-Tay.

HW Entertainment licenses verses from its catalog, handles Custom requests to artists’ teams, and runs promotion and billboard bookings for independent artists. We show how our licenses are structured before you pay.

HW Entertainment LLC is credited as the label on Rappin’ 4-Tay releases on public streaming stores, including “Playaz Circle” (2023). See the Rappin’ 4-Tay listings on Qobuz.
